Assistive Pepper Robot on NVIDIA Jetson

2026

A companion robot for patients and caregivers. When someone walks in, Pepper recognizes them, says hello, checks in, and offers an activity: Simon Says, a guess-the-object game, or a seated exercise routine. All the AI runs locally on a Jetson attached to a robot that is more than ten years old.

  • Live video from the robot to the Jetson, with YOLOv8 object detection and local face recognition (SCRFD + AuraFace).
  • Root-caused corrupted camera frames to YUV422 decoding, and a torchvision::nms crash to a CUDA kernel missing from Jetson's aarch64 build.
  • Pepper refused to go through doorways. The fix was smaller steps, not a bigger push.
  • Bridged Pepper's navigation to ROS 2 / Nav2; tested with pytest plus a hardware-in-the-loop checklist.

Assistive Cart Robot

2026 · Gazebo simulation

A shopping assistant that picks items off supermarket shelves: a UR5 arm with a Robotiq 2F-85 gripper on a 4-wheel cart. It grasps using only two fingertip contact sensors, with no camera in the loop.

  • Reactive grasp state machine: when only one finger touches the can, the gripper shifts toward that side to re-center before closing and lifting.
  • 3-level LiDAR safety filter for teleoperation (emergency stop, directional hard stops, slow zones) that fails safe to zero velocity if the scan drops.
  • PyQt5 operator GUI and a one-command bring-up with 7 Docker Compose services.

LLM-Driven Design Loop

2025 · BrainSpec Lab, NC State

A closed loop where a language model writes C/C++ hardware policies, a simulator benchmarks them, and the results go back to the model so it improves the next version, with no hand-tuning in between. This is the project that made me forget to sleep, and the loop I want to bring to robots next.

  • Cache replacement policy: +10.41% over baseline across 5 industry-standard workloads.
  • L1 instruction prefetcher (IPC-1 framework): IPC from 0.950 to 0.981.
